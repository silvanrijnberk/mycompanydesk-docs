To preview, print, or download a quote PDF:
1. Open the quote
2. On a computer, the quote preview is already on the page. Click **Full screen** to see it large; the preview header also has buttons to print and to download the PDF
3. On your phone, open the three-dot menu and choose **Preview** or **Download PDF**
4. In the large preview, use **Print** or **Download PDF**
5. Return to the quote if changes are needed

Tip: The preview uses your current invoice design. To change the layout, click **Change design** next to the preview.
