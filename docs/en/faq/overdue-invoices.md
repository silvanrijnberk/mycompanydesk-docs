To view and manage overdue invoices:
1. Go to Invoices
2. The **Overdue** group sits at the top of the list. To see only those invoices, choose the status "Overdue" under **Filters**
3. Click the Due date column to sort by due date
4. Open an invoice to send a reminder or review its detail page

The **Awaiting payment** tab also shows at a glance how many invoices are overdue, and the dashboard links you back to overdue invoices.
