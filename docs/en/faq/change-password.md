To change your password:
1. Go to Settings → "Inloggen" (signing in)
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter and confirm your new password
4. Click "Change password"

After the change, your own login address receives an email: your password has been changed. If that email arrives while you did nothing, contact support right away.

Tip: Use a strong unique password and avoid reusing passwords from other services.
