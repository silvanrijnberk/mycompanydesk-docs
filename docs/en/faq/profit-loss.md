The profit and loss statement lives in your bookkeeping (Office):
1. Open **Bookkeeping** in the sidebar, under Finance
2. Click **Accountant view** to show the tabs meant for your accountant
3. Open the **Profit & loss** tab: your revenue and costs per ledger account, with a chart of how the result builds up
4. Pick the period you want to inspect with the period and year picker

Just want a quick look at your profit? It also sits on Reports › Overview and on the **Figures** page. There, expenses are counted without VAT, capitalised investments are recognised through their depreciation schedule rather than in the purchase month, and bank or inbox drafts that are still pending review are excluded because they are not in the books yet.
