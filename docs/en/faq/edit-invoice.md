To edit an invoice:
1. Open the invoice from the list
2. Click "Edit"; this is available while the invoice is a draft or finalized but not yet sent
3. Make your changes to line items, customer, dates, or terms
4. Save the updated invoice

Once an invoice has left your desk it is locked and can no longer be edited: that covers an invoice that was sent or emailed, marked as paid, or whose PDF you downloaded or shared. The page shows which one applies (downloaded or shared, but never emailed, gets its own note: Invoice locked: already with your customer) and the legally required details become read-only. A credit note plus, where needed, a new invoice is the way to change things; a finalized invoice that never reached the customer can be edited as usual.

Tip: Need to fix a mistake on a sent invoice? A credit note plus a new invoice keeps your numbering and VAT records clean.
