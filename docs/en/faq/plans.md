MyCompanyDesk has two plans: **Desk** and **Office**.

**Desk** is free and stays free. You get:
- unlimited invoices, quotes and expenses
- projects and time registration
- your own website on `yourname.mycompanydesk.site`, with the "Made with MyCompanyDesk" badge
- your own logo and branding on invoices and quotes; the documents themselves carry no MyCompanyDesk mark
- exports to PDF, Excel/CSV and a full backup
- reading and replying to incoming mail in your inbox
- sending a payment reminder by hand
- preparing your VAT return and filing it yourself with the Dutch tax authority
- each month 3 AI receipt scans, 10 assistant messages and 10 AI suggestions
- 100 MB of storage
- one business of your own, plus free access for your accountant

On Desk, the emails to your customers and your payment and signing pages carry a small "Made with MyCompanyDesk".

When customers pay an invoice online through Mollie or Stripe, Desk holds back a €0.50 service fee including VAT from your payout for each payment of €5 or more, on top of the costs of Mollie or Stripe. Your customer does not pay it. On Office there is no service fee.

**Office** costs €12.99 per month or €129.90 per year, excluding 21% VAT. If your workspace has never had a paid subscription and you choose yearly billing, the first year costs €35.88 (€2.99 per month). After that it renews at €129.90 per year. The introductory price does not apply to monthly billing.

Office includes everything in Desk, plus:
- contracts and assets
- recurring invoices and expenses, and automatic invoicing of hours and contracts
- automatic payment reminders
- full bookkeeping (ledger, balance sheet, profit & loss and annual accounts), advanced reports and AI insights
- the full business inbox: writing new mail, mailboxes, AI and rules
- custom domains (up to 5) and newsletters
- connecting your bank (up to 3 bank connections)
- giving people access to your workspace, as many as you like and with no per-person charge, with advanced permissions
- multiple businesses of your own
- automatic delivery to your accountant and e-invoicing via Peppol
- privacy mode, language tools and priority support
- no "Made with MyCompanyDesk" on your payment page, signing page and emails
- unlimited storage, and each month 200 receipt scans, 1,000 assistant messages, 2,000 AI suggestions, 15,000 sent and 20,000 received emails

See the [plans page](https://mycompanydesk.nl/plans) for the full overview.

**More businesses and accounting firms**
- Do you own more than one business, for example a holding company next to your operating company? Each additional business of your own costs €5.99 per month or €59.90 per year on top of Office. Office covers up to 10 businesses of your own.
- When an accounting firm takes over the bill for a client, the firm pays €6.99 per month or €69.90 per year per client.
- Access for your accountant is free on every plan.

**Trial**
- Every new account gets 60 days of Office for free, with no credit card. If you sign up through your accountant's link and they are a MyCompanyDesk partner, the trial lasts 12 months.
- If you do not choose a plan when the trial ends, your workspace moves to Desk automatically. There is no paywall: your data stays and you simply keep working.

**Upgrading and downgrading**
- You can switch between Desk and Office at any time under Settings › **Your subscription**.
- When you upgrade, the new features are available right away.
- When you downgrade from Office to Desk, Office-only features stop working: your bank stops importing, new recurring invoices or expenses are no longer generated, automatic payment reminders stop, the reports, your bookkeeping with the annual accounts and your income tax return stop, and you can no longer write new mail yourself, read your mail in an email app or manage your domain. Contracts, assets and newsletters open again as soon as you switch Office back on, and receipt scanning and storage go back to the Desk limits. Emails to customers and the payment and signing pages carry the "Made with MyCompanyDesk" badge again, and a domain you received free with Office is renewed by you from then on. Email keeps coming in (including on your own domain addresses), you can still read and reply, and invoices and replies on Desk still go out from your own address. Your website stays online and stays editable, you can still prepare your VAT return and file it yourself, and your data stays in your workspace. The cancel page lists exactly what keeps working and what stops before you decide.

**Billing**
- All prices are shown excluding 21% VAT. The amount you pay at checkout includes VAT, which you can reclaim as input VAT (voorbelasting).
- There is no per-user charge. Desk is for one user plus free accountant access. Office allows unlimited users plus free accountant access.
- You can cancel or downgrade whenever you want. Not satisfied after your first payment? You get your money back within 14 days.
