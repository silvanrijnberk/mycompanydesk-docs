You can use MyCompanyDesk on your phone with the app or in your mobile browser.

**With the app**
1. Install the MyCompanyDesk app from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone)
2. Sign in with the same account you use on your computer
3. Turn on push notifications if you want to know right away when something happens, see [Notification settings](/en/faq/notification-settings)

**In your browser**
1. Open your mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.)
2. Go to [app.mycompanydesk.com](https://app.mycompanydesk.com)
3. The app is responsive and adapts to your screen size
4. You can add it to your home screen for quick access

Tip: Every feature is available on mobile, so you can create invoices, record expenses and manage customers on the go.
