Contracts are part of Office. If Contracts is not in your menu, turn it on under Settings › "Modules".

To create a contract:
1. Go to Contracts and click "Add contract"
2. Under "Subject", choose Service, Rental, or Collaboration
3. Choose the customer. For Rental, also choose the property you rent out
4. Enter the contract name, choose under "How is the price agreed?" how you bill (fixed amount, hourly rate, daily rate, or fixed project price), and enter the amount, VAT rate, and recurrence
5. Set the start date and the first invoice date, then save

Tip: Want the invoices to be created for you? Turn on "Automatic invoicing" on the saved contract's page and choose whether the invoice waits as a draft, is emailed to the customer right away, or is collected by direct debit.
