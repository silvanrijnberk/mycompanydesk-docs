To view a customer's payment history:
1. Open Customers and select the customer
2. Check the "History" card on the customer profile; it lists this customer's latest invoices, quotes, projects, and hours, each with its number or name, date, status, and amount
3. Click "View all" for all of this customer's invoices, and open an invoice for the payment details, such as when and how it was paid

The invoice detail page is where the full payment information lives; the customer profile gives you the quick status overview.
