To add, edit, or remove line items on an invoice:
1. Open the invoice in edit mode (or create a new one)
2. Click "Add Line" or the + button to add a new row
3. Enter description, quantity, unit price, and VAT rate. The description field is the way into your catalog: click the empty field (or press Arrow Down) to see your catalog at once, or start typing and the matching items appear underneath. Choosing one fills the line with its description, price and VAT rate
4. Enter keeps your own text; to pick a suggestion, click it or move with the arrow keys and Enter
5. Typed something longer? On a new document, when the first (and only) line describes a whole job in six words or more and AI is on, the list offers **Draft lines from this text**: the sentence turns into priced lines, from your catalog where possible
6. A plain hand-typed line with a description and a price above zero shows **Save to your catalog** underneath, so your catalog grows while you invoice
7. To remove a line, click the trash icon next to it
8. Save the invoice when done

The same description-field suggestions appear on quote and recurring invoice lines.

Tip: Use clear, specific descriptions for each line item to avoid customer questions.
