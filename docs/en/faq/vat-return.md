To check and file your VAT return in the app:
1. Open **Taxes** in the sidebar, under Finance
2. The tabs at the top are **Overview** (what still needs doing before you file), **Return** (the boxes for the chosen period), **Checks**, **Transactions** (the invoices and expenses behind the amounts), **Corrections** and **Periods**
3. Pick the period you want to check and open related invoices or expenses if you need to inspect the source records
4. Click **File return**. MyCompanyDesk records the return with the amount per box. Then file it in Mijn Belastingdienst Zakelijk (the **Open Mijn Belastingdienst Zakelijk** button takes you there) and click **Mark as filed**
5. Use the export options under Taxes if you need the figures outside the app

Preparing your VAT return and filing it yourself works on every plan.
