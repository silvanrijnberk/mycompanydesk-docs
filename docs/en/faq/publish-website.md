# Publish your website and put it online

Your website only goes online once you publish it. Until then you see your changes in the editor, but visitors do not.

## How do I publish my website?

1. Click **Website** in the menu on the left.
2. Click **Edit site**. The editor opens.
3. Change your texts, photos and pages. Everything is saved automatically.
4. Click **Publiceer** (Publish) at the top right, then **Publiceer nu** (Publish now).

You get the message **Gepubliceerd** (Published) and your site is live. The editor buttons are currently shown in Dutch, even when your app is set to English. You can also publish without opening the editor: on **Website** → **Overview**, the status card has a **Publish** button as well.

Publishing works on every plan, including Desk (free).

## My website is not online yet, what now?

If **Website** → **Overview** says "Not online yet" or "Not published yet", you have never published. Open the editor via **Edit site** and click **Publiceer** (Publish). Your site does not have to be finished first: you can always publish again later.

Sometimes a warning appears first when you publish, for example "Your site still has placeholder text", "Legal pages are missing company details" or "Your website shows your login e-mail address". These warnings do not stop you. Choose **Edit first** to fix it, or **Publish anyway** to go live straight away.

## What does "ready to publish" mean?

Everything you change in the editor is saved as a draft. Until you publish again, visitors still see the previous version. You can tell by:

- a dot on the **Publiceer** (Publish) button in the editor;
- the text "klaar om te publiceren" (ready to publish) at the bottom of the editor;
- "1 change ready to publish" (or more) on **Website** → **Overview**.

Click **Publiceer** (Publish) to put everything live in one go. If you would rather throw your changes away, choose **Revert to the live version** in the three-dot menu.

Pages set to **Draft** stay hidden, even after publishing. Set a page to **Live** if it should be included.

## What address is my website on?

- **Free address:** when you first publish, your site gets a free address based on your business name, such as `yourbusiness.mycompanydesk.site`. Until then you see "Your address will appear once you publish".
- **Your own domain:** want your site on your own domain name, such as `yourbusiness.com`? That comes with Office. Go to **Website** → **Domain & email** and follow the steps. See [Connect your own domain and nameservers](/en/faq/connect-domain).

## How do I take my website offline?

On **Website**, click the three dots at the top right and choose **Take website offline**. Confirm with **Take offline**. Visitors then see a message that the site is temporarily unavailable. Your published version is kept: **Bring website back online** puts it live again straight away.

Do not leave your site offline for long: after a few days Google drops it from the search results, and it takes weeks to come back.
