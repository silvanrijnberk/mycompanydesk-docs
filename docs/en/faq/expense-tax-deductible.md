To track deductibility in the app:
1. Open the expense and click "Edit"
2. Switch the "VAT deductible" toggle on or off. Turn it off if you (also) use the purchase privately
3. Check the category and VAT settings on the same expense. Some categories limit the VAT deduction; the form then shows this
4. Save the expense and use report filters to review the recorded totals

The app can record this setting, but it does not decide whether an expense is legally deductible.
