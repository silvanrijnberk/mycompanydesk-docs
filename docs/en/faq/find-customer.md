To find a customer:
1. Go to Customers
2. Use the search bar at the top; it matches the customer name, customer number, email, city, contact person, KVK number, and phone number
3. Phone search uses the digits only: 06 also finds +316 and the other way around
4. Results filter in real-time as you type

Tip: The list is always in name order, with favorites pinned to the top. Mark customers you work with often as favorites (the star) so they are always at hand.
