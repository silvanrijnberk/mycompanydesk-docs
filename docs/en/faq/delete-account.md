To delete your account:
1. Download your data first at Settings › "Download data" if you want to keep a copy. **Download everything** gives you one ZIP with your records, including the PDFs and receipts
2. Go to Settings › "Cancel account"
3. Under "Delete account permanently", click "Delete"
4. Confirm with your current password. Sign in via Google, Microsoft or Apple, and there is no password field; you confirm without one.

What happens next depends on your role:

- **Not the owner of a workspace**: your account and all your personal data are erased for good. If you sign in via Google, Microsoft or Apple without a password of your own, the app asks you to set one first. You do that under Settings › "Login" with the "Stuur link om een wachtwoord in te stellen" (send a link to set a password) button; after that, deletion works here as usual.
- **Owner of a workspace**: your request schedules the deletion of your account and the whole workspace. Nothing is erased right away: for the first 30 days you can still log in, download your records and cancel the deletion, and you get an email when the request is placed and again a week before the date. After that, the account, the workspace and everything in it are deleted: invoices, customers, expenses, mail, website and files. Your accountant and anyone else with access to your records are told and lose access; the accounts of your team members are deleted with the workspace. You must keep your own records for years, so download before you confirm: after deletion we no longer have them.

The deletion also settles your subscription the way Cancel does: it does not renew and will not be charged again. Have you just paid for Office for the first time? Then the money-back guarantee in [Plan & payments](/en/settings/billing) applies and you get your first payment back in full; Office stops right away. A canceled deletion restores your subscription as it was.

The same page also lets you cancel only your paid subscription. You then stop paying, but your account and data stay, and the records you must keep for your administration stay in the app for you. Before you confirm, the page lists what keeps working and what stops, so you know what you are choosing. The subscription card and the data wipe belong to the workspace admin; a member who opens the page finds only the account-deletion card, enough to remove their own account. Deleting your account removes your account and all your data for good.

Important: account deletion cannot be undone. Export everything you need before you proceed.
