# PDF design

Make your invoice, quote and reminder PDFs match your brand. Two settings pages control the look, and one style applies to all your documents.

- **Logo and colour**: your logo and brand colour, the basis of everything.
- **Invoice design**: the design studio where you style the document itself, with a live preview.

Both live under **Settings**. See the [settings overview](/en/settings/) for the full map.

## Logo and colour

Go to **Settings** and open **Logo and colour**. This page sets your brand identity:

- **Display name:** the name customers see, if it differs from your registered company name.
- **Brand colour:** pick any colour, or start from one of the presets. This colour is used on your website, newsletter, emails, invoices and quotes. A warning appears when a colour is too light to stay readable. If you choose a separate accent colour in Invoice design, invoices, quotes, emails and the payment page follow that colour instead. You can adopt that accent as your brand colour in one click.
- **Company Logo:** upload an SVG (recommended), PNG or JPG; PNG and JPG go up to 10 MB, SVG stays at 2 MB, and MyCompanyDesk downsizes an oversized raster image itself on upload. For photo-style logos there is a one-click background remover. No logo yet? **Generate Logo from Text** creates a simple one from your initials and a background colour, right on this page.
- **Preview:** a sample header shows your logo and colour together, so you see the result before it reaches a customer.

Changes save automatically.

## Invoice design

Go to **Settings** and open **Invoice design**. This is the design studio for your documents: every control on the left, a live A4 preview on the right that updates as you click. The preview works with your real settings: the payment term, the quote validity and the next invoice number it shows are the ones you have set.

The quickest route is **Create a design from my branding** with the **Use my branding** button: it builds a complete design around the brand colour you chose under Logo and colour. Not happy? **Try another** cycles through different looks. Everything stays editable afterwards:

- **Logo**: upload or replace your logo right in the studio (**Change logo**). Without a logo, your company name sits at the top, and your logo also appears in your emails and on your payment page.
- **Starting point**: seven templates: Classic, Modern, Compact, Statement, Editorial, Atelier and Botanical. Changing any option afterwards turns the design into your own custom variant.
- **Accent colour**: your brand colour is offered first. When you have a logo, colours sampled from it appear as ready-made swatches. There is also a set of curated shades, and a custom picker for any colour. A different accent colour overrides your brand colour on invoices, quotes, emails and the payment page.
- **Paper tint**: Wit (white) or one of the soft off-white tones (Crème, Greige, Zand, Mist), named in Dutch in the app.
- **Font**: a heading font (Marcellus, Playfair Display, Fraunces or Cormorant) and a text font (Inter, Lato or EB Garamond).
- **Theme / layout**: the overall arrangement of the document, plus the **Line spacing** (Compact, Normaal and Ruim, as the app names them). The Botanical layout has optional decorative artwork you can switch off.
- **Template per document type**: every document type follows your invoice design, and where one must differ you give it its own template. The work order is the example: it can follow your invoice layout or carry its own design, and a work order has no closing text.
- **Text size**: Small, Normal or Large for the text of your document. Headings and your company name keep their size.
- **Your own letterhead**: the studio starts with one choice, under **Layout**: **Our design**, the templates, colour and fonts described in the bullets above, or **Your own letterhead**, the letterhead your printer made. The two are not combined: under your letterhead the invoice renders in a plain classical arrangement, without artwork or paper tint, and the design you kept under Our design stays saved, so switching back costs nothing. Have letterhead from a printer? Upload it as a PDF, PNG or JPG, filling the whole page of portrait A4. The studio measures how much space the printwork takes at the top and bottom and reserves it, so nothing lands on top of your artwork; adjust the spacing if the measure is off. **Your customer's address**: put the address on the left or the right of the page, so it lines up with the window of your envelope. Is your logo already in the letterhead? One switch leaves out the logo and name at the top of the document; your address always stays, it is required. Colours sampled from the upload appear as ready-made swatches in the accent colour picker, so headings and totals match the paper. **First page only** is on by default: your letterhead prints on the first page, and the following pages are a plain sheet with your name or logo and a page number. Turn the switch off to put your letterhead on every page. Is the document title, say "Invoice", already printed on the letterhead? The switch **"Invoice is already on the letterhead"** leaves that title out; each document type is set separately, so a quote whose paper says Invoice keeps its own title.
- **Texts on your document**: the closing text, ready under "Thank you" on every new invoice (a quote without its own text gets the text of your invoices), and the payment instruction shown under Payment on new invoices and on your payment page, with {iban}, {company} and {invoiceNumber} filled in for you.
- **Footer**: the line at the bottom of every document, for example your KVK number or a thank-you note.

**Also on your invoice**: below the controls, a card lists what appears on your invoice but is set elsewhere: your company details, payment methods, payment term, invoice numbering, quote validity and VAT settings. Each row shows the current value and opens the settings page where it lives; the back button there returns you to the studio.

Changes save automatically and show in the preview within a moment. Want the standard look back? **Disable theme (back to default)** returns your documents to the default design.

## One style for all documents

The design applies to every PDF MyCompanyDesk creates for you: invoices, quotes, payment reminders and work orders. Each document type follows your invoice design; give any of them its own template when it needs to differ. The colours customers see on emails and the payment page follow the same accent colour. You style it once; every document type stays consistent. Your letterhead shows up wherever the document appears: the PDF, your customer's view in the customer portal and the work order. Documents that run over multiple pages number their sheets: "Page 2 of 3" sits quietly in the bottom margin of every page.

## Language

Document PDFs are available in Dutch, English, German and French. Documents from a Dutch workspace are produced in Dutch by default, so the labels on your invoices (like "Factuur" and "Vervaldatum") match what your customers expect.

## Logo tips

For the best result on PDFs:

- **Format:** SVG gives the best quality and stays at 2 MB; PNG (preferably with a transparent background) and JPG also work, up to 10 MB
- **Resolution:** at least 300 pixels wide
- **Shape:** landscape or square logos fit the layouts best
