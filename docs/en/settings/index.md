# Settings overview

Everything you can configure in MyCompanyDesk lives on a single settings page. Open **Settings** from the menu, or go to `/settings`. Every topic is a full-width row, sorted into six plain-language groups, and each setting is at most two clicks away. A few rows show their current state on the right, such as your theme, how many notifications are on, your brand colour or your plan.

There is no longer a separate tree for company settings and personal settings. Personal topics and business topics sit on the same page; the group headers make clear which is which. Old bookmarked settings links redirect to the matching new page automatically.

## About you

Personal settings. They apply to your own account and never change anything for other people in your workspace.

| Row | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Your details** | Your name, email address and photo |
| **Login** | Password, two-step verification, passkeys and logging out |
| **Login codes** | Your two-step login codes, generated by the app itself. This row only appears in the installed app. |
| **Notifications** | Which events we message you about, plus push notifications on this device |
| **Display** | Light or dark theme, text size, app language and how much you see above lists (dense or calm) |

See [Your personal settings](/en/account/profile) for a walkthrough of these pages.

## Your business

Shared settings. Changes here apply to everyone in your workspace.

| Row | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Business details** | Name, address, KvK and VAT number, and your opening hours; these appear on your invoices, quotes and website |
| **Logo and colour** | Your logo and brand colour, the basis for your invoices, quotes, emails and website |
| **Modules** | Switch parts of the app on or off, such as quotes, work orders, hours, receipt scanning, contracts, projects and newsletters |
| **People with access** | Invite colleagues or a partner and set what they can see and do. Team members are part of Office, unlimited and with no per-person charge. |
| **Your accountant** | Give your accountant access, add a contact person and set up automatic forwarding. Accountant access is free on every plan. |

## Invoices and money

| Row | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Invoices and quotes** | Payment term, automatic reminders, numbering and how long quotes stay valid |
| **Invoice design** | Layout, colour and typeface of your invoices, with a live preview |
| **Payments** | How customers can pay you: your IBAN, iDEAL and PayPal |
| **VAT** | Your usual VAT rate, how often you file and the KOR small-business scheme |
| **Automation** | Everything that happens automatically: reminders, booking from your bank and inbox, e-invoicing. Most automatic steps are part of Office. |
| **Connect bank account** | Connect your bank so transactions come in and expenses are recognised automatically (Office) |

## Customers, mail and website

| Row | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **What your customer sees** | View every email and page your customer receives from you |
| **Customer portal** | Welcome text, display and how customers get in |
| **Ask for reviews** | Automatically ask customers for a review on Google or Trustpilot |
| **Email** | Addresses, texts and design of your emails, plus rules for incoming mail |
| **Your website and domain** | Your own site and domain name. A custom domain is part of Office. |

## Expenses and hours

| Row | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Expenses** | Default category and payment method for new expenses, plus the address for forwarding receipts by email |
| **Expense categories** | Add, edit, archive or delete custom expense categories; set VAT treatment and deduction percentage |
| **Hours & calendar** | Working hours, entry behaviour, hourly rate, rounding and travel settings |
| **Switching** | Bring over outstanding invoices, revenue and the opening balance from your old package |

## Subscription and data

| Row | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Your subscription** | What you pay and what is included |
| **My requests** | View and manage your support requests |
| **Download data** | Everything you have stored, in one file |
| **Clear data** | Start over without cancelling your account |
| **Cancel account** | Stop using MyCompanyDesk |

## Admin-only rows

Six rows are only visible to workspace admins: **People with access**, **Your accountant**, **Connect bank account**, **Your subscription**, **Clear data** and **Cancel account**. If you were invited as a team member without admin rights, these rows are hidden for you; ask the workspace admin to make those changes.

Your role also decides what you see. When you work as an accountant in a client's books, the rows about running that business (such as Business details, Logo and colour, Email and Modules) disappear, and you keep your own personal settings plus the bookkeeping work, such as VAT and Expenses. A team member only sees the rows for pages they have access to.

## Search

Press **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows and Linux), or click the search box at the top of the page. Typing filters the rows as you type, and everyday words work too: "iban" finds **Payments**, "dark" finds **Display**, "return" finds **VAT**.


## The Settings button on a work page

Pages that show things configured elsewhere carry a **Settings** button in the top bar: invoices, quotes, recurring invoices, contracts, customers, your Catalog, expenses, bookkeeping, taxes, the inbox, newsletters, your website, and hours and calendar. Open it and a panel lists exactly the settings that govern what you see on that page, each with its current value next to it: the payment term with reminders, the next invoice number, the deposit percentage, whether a logo or IBAN is set, your VAT rate and how often you file. A setting with no value yet shows an add action instead, so an empty spot is visible at a glance. A row click opens the card on the settings page, and the back button there returns you to where you were. **All settings** opens the full overview at `/settings`.

The button does not appear on new and edit forms, nor on the settings pages themselves. A page whose contents are not configured anywhere does not get one either.

## Where do I find...

| I want to... | Open |
|---|---|
| Change my name or photo | **Your details** |
| Change my password or set up two-step verification | **Login** |
| Choose what I get notified about | **Notifications** |
| Switch to dark mode, larger text, another app language or hide the summary cards above lists | **Display** |
| Fix my company address or VAT number | **Business details** |
| Upload a logo or change my brand colour | **Logo and colour** |
| Change the payment term or reminder emails | **Invoices and quotes** |
| Restyle my invoice PDF | **Invoice design** |
| Add my IBAN or let customers pay with iDEAL | **Payments** |
| Change my VAT rate or filing frequency | **VAT** |
| See what my customer receives from me | **What your customer sees** |
| Send invoices from my own email address | **Email** |
| Set up my website or domain | **Your website and domain** |
| Turn quotes, projects or newsletters on or off | **Modules** |
| Invite a colleague | **People with access** (admins) |
| Give my accountant access | **Your accountant** (admins) |
| Change time-tracking options | **Hours & calendar** |
| Upgrade or check my plan | **Your subscription** (admins) |
| Connect my bank | **Connect bank account** (admins) |
| Add or edit expense categories | **Expense categories** |
| Bring over data from my old bookkeeping package | **Switching** |
| Download a copy of my data | **Download data** |
| Wipe my business data | **Clear data** (admins) |
| Cancel my account | **Cancel account** (admins) |

Calendar sync with Google or Outlook is also listed under **Settings** › **Hours & calendar**. You can still open it directly from the hours and calendar page.

## Old links

Settings URLs from earlier versions of the app, including the old separate workspace and personal settings trees, redirect to the matching new page. Bookmarks and links in old emails keep working.
