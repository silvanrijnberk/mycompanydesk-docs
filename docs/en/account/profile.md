# Your personal settings

Your personal settings live in the same Settings area as everything else. Open **Settings** and look at the **About you** group at the top: **Your details**, **Login**, **Notifications** and **Display** (the installed app also shows **Login codes** there). These rows only affect your own account, never your teammates. Changes on these pages save automatically while you type.

See the [Settings overview](/en/settings/) for the full settings page.

## Your details

Your name, email address, profile photo and what goes under your emails.

- **First name** and **Last name**. Your name also appears on your invoices.
- **Email address**. This is the address you sign in with. It is read-only here; **Change your email address** takes you to **Login**, where you change it yourself. See [Change email](/en/faq/change-email).
- **Job title** and **Phone**. Optional.
- **Sign-off on your e-mails**. This text is added under the emails you send.
- **Profile photo**. Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP image up to 5 MB and crop it before it goes live: drag the photo to move it and zoom until your head sits in the circle. Customers see it on emails you send. If you create your account with Google or Microsoft, we adopt your profile photo from there automatically; that only happens at sign-up, so replacing or removing the photo later is always your call. A photo is optional, and you can remove it again at any time.

## Display

How the app looks for you, on every device you sign in on.

- **Theme**: Light, Dark or Automatic. Automatic follows your device: if your phone or computer is set to dark, the app is dark too.
- **Text size**: Normal, Large or Extra large, for more comfortable reading. The choice is saved per device, so your phone can use a larger size than your laptop. The app also follows your phone's system text-size setting up to the Extra Large step; at that size the mobile bottom tab bar switches to icons-only to keep all tabs reachable.
- **App language**: Dutch, English, German or French. This changes the menus and buttons for you only; the language of your invoices and quotes is a separate choice per document.
- **Advanced mode**: a per-device switch that reveals extra technical options across the app, such as DNS and SSL controls on the domain pages. Leave it off unless you need those.
- **Figures above your lists**: choose **Calm** or **Detailed**. Calm is the default: you only see the list, without summary tiles or side cards. Choose **Detailed** if you want summaries and totals above and beside your lists. The choice only applies on the device where you make it, so your phone and your laptop can differ.

## Notifications

Choose what MyCompanyDesk messages you about. The switches sit in groups: **Every week** (on Monday, what needs doing that week), **Your money** (invoices that need your attention, money coming in, deadlines such as your VAT return), **Your customers** (quote requests, signed quotes and new mail in your inbox), **Your team** (only when you work with others) and **From MyCompanyDesk** (new features and tips, at most one email per month). Each row shows how the message reaches you: **In the app**, **By email** or **On your phone**.

Next to the switches, **Where does mail from MyCompanyDesk arrive?** lets you pick the address these emails go to.

Truly important messages about your account are always sent and cannot be switched off.

Below the switches, **Enable push notifications** turns on push notifications for this device, so overdue invoices, payments and deadlines also reach you as notifications on your phone or computer. Your browser or phone will ask for permission the first time. If you skip it, you can always turn it on later from the notification bell at the top of the app.

Push notifications work in browsers and in the app for Android and iPhone. On Android the notification uses the app icon and category colour; for inbox messages it also shows the sender's avatar when the app has registered it. Older Android builds without the native messaging service still receive the standard SDK-rendered notification.

## Connected calendars

You can connect **Google Calendar** or **Outlook** so appointments from your calendar also appear in MyCompanyDesk. You find it under **Settings** › **Hours & calendar** › **Connect calendar (Google or Outlook)**, and from the **Schedule** page. Each connection can be removed again from the same page.

## Customer locations

The **Customer locations** card under **Settings** › **Notifications** shows how many of your addressed customers can be placed on a map. Location features, such as finding nearby customers, only work for customers whose address has been resolved to coordinates.

The card lists:

- how many customers with an address are resolved
- how many still need to be looked up
- the **Update addresses** button to look up addresses in one go
- a short list of customers whose addresses could not be resolved, so you can fix them

When you edit a customer address, the cached coordinates are cleared automatically. The next lookup will try to resolve the new address.
