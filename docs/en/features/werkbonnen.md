# Work orders

A work order records what you did at a customer's: the visit, the hours, the materials, and a signature from the customer confirming the work was done. Once the work order is signed, you turn it into an invoice with one click. It is made for work you carry out on location, such as installation, maintenance or repairs.

## Turning it on

Work orders are an opt-in module. Switch them on under **Settings > Modules**. There is no plan gate: the module is free on every plan, and access follows the same page permission as invoices.

## The work order list

The list lives at **Work orders** in the navigation. Tabs at the top filter by state: **All**, **Draft**, **Signed** and **Invoiced**. Each row shows the work order number, the customer, the work address and the state as a coloured pill. The search box finds work orders by number, customer or address.

## Creating a work order

1. Choose **New work order**
2. Pick the **customer** (search existing customers or add a new one)
3. Check the **work address**. It is taken from the customer, and you can change it when you worked somewhere else
4. Fill in the **date** and the start and end time
5. Describe the **work carried out**
6. Add lines under **Labour** (hours with an hourly rate) and **Materials** (quantity with a unit price and VAT)
7. A **discount** per line works the same way as on quotes and invoices
8. The **internal note** is just for you: it never appears on the work order or the invoice

Save the work order, or choose **Save and get it signed** right away. You can also create a work order from a quote: the lines are pre-filled from the quote.

## Getting it signed

On the work order page, choose **Get it signed**. Fill in the name of the person signing and let the customer sign with a finger or stylus on your phone. By signing, the customer confirms that the work shown on the work order was carried out.

Once signed, the work order can no longer be changed. The signature, the signer's name and the signing date appear on the work order itself.

## PDF and email

Every work order has a PDF with the visit details, the hours and materials, the totals and, when it is signed, the customer's signature. Email the signed work order to your customer from the work order page: the PDF is attached automatically.

## From work order to invoice

A signed work order turns into an invoice in one click. The invoice starts as a draft with the hours and materials already on the lines, and it shows the work order number as a reference. There is one invoice per work order; if it already exists, the link on the work order page takes you straight to it. Check the draft invoice and send it as usual.

## Customer merge

When you merge two customers, their work orders move to the remaining customer together with the rest of their records.

## Tips

- Work orders are draft until they are signed. Use the state tabs to see what still needs a signature.
- The work order page shows the next step for each state: fill in and sign, then invoice or email, then check the invoice.
- Create a work order from a quote and the lines carry over: hour lines become labour, the other lines become materials, and line discounts come along.
