# Wholesalers

Connect the wholesaler you buy materials from, then add their articles to your quotes, invoices and work orders with your own purchase price and your markup. MyCompanyDesk only reads the cart you send back: while you are putting a document together, you order nothing. Connecting a wholesaler and adding their materials is part of Office; on Desk, the tip explains what Office unlocks.

A wholesaler is a suppliers feature. You connect the account you already have with the wholesaler, or fill in the details your supplier gave you for their ordering connection. Nothing changes at the wholesaler: their webshop keeps working the way it always did.

## Connecting a supplier

The overview lives at **Settings › Wholesalers**. Click **Add supplier** and pick from the list: it includes suppliers such as Technische Unie, Rexel, Solar, Isero and Jongeneel, plus job-list suppliers such as Bouwmaat and Alpheios, grouped by sector. Your supplier is not on the list? Choose **Other supplier**: any supplier with an OCI connection (PunchOut, sometimes called web selection) works.

Fill in the details your supplier gave you: customer number, username, password and the **entry address**, the web address for the OCI login. The password is stored encrypted and is only used to open the supplier's webshop for you. Changing the entry address asks for the password again. Disconnecting a supplier wipes the username and password. Suppliers without a logo show a grey warehouse icon. A supplier you can no longer connect stays in the list with that note until you disconnect it.

## Materials on a quote, invoice or work order

On a quote, invoice or work order, the **Materials from wholesaler** button next to your own lines opens the picker. Choose the supplier and open their webshop, put the articles you need in the cart, and send the cart back to MyCompanyDesk. Nothing is ordered: the app waits for the cart, then shows the lines for review, with article, quantity, purchase price, markup and selling price. A line whose price did not come through, you type the price yourself; a price in another currency is noted as such and is not converted. Choose the lines to add, optionally also save them in the **Catalog** so you can find them again later, and they land on the document. Under the lines you see what the cart costs and what you earn on it; your customer only ever sees the selling price.

## Job lists (Bouwmaat)

Bouwmaat has no OCI login. Instead, put your materials in a job list on bouwmaat.nl or in the Bouwmaat app, choose **Share job list** there, and paste the link into your quote, invoice or work order under **Materials from wholesaler**. The lines come in with Bouwmaat's webshop prices, excluding VAT. Did not every line make it in? The app tells you, so nothing goes missing unnoticed.

## Markup

The markup turns your purchase price into a selling price. Every supplier carries a **default markup on purchase price** that you fill in when connecting, and you can still adjust the markup per line. For materials you type in yourself, the general default lives under **Settings › Invoices and quotes** (see [Markup](/en/features/invoices#markup)). The margin is visible to the owner only and never prints on the document.

## Purchase invoices via Peppol

Want the purchase invoices of your wholesaler to land in Expenses by themselves? Switch on Peppol under **Settings › Invoices and quotes** and ask the wholesaler to send their invoices via Peppol. The connection window mentions this tip as well.
