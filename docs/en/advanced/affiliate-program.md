# Affiliate Program

The MyCompanyDesk affiliate program is for independent publishers, comparison sites, blogs, newsletters, and other websites that want to earn a reward by referring visitors who start a paid MyCompanyDesk subscription.

## Who can join

The program is open to external publishers that send relevant traffic to MyCompanyDesk. You do not need a MyCompanyDesk account to take part.

## How to apply

Apply with the form at [mycompanydesk.nl/affiliate](https://mycompanydesk.nl/affiliate). You enter the name of your site or newsletter, your email address, optionally your website, and what you write about. We review every application ourselves and let you know within a few working days whether you are in.

Once approved, you get your own link with your code in it and your own stats page. No login is needed: the page opens from a personal link.

## How it works

1. Share your link with your audience. It points to the MyCompanyDesk website, so visitors can look around first.
2. A visitor who arrives through your link stays attributed to your code for the rest of that browser session, even if they read a few pages before signing up.
3. If the visitor starts a paid subscription, a reward is created for you. A free sign-up earns nothing.

Signing up through your own link does not count: a workspace registered with the same email address as your affiliate application earns no reward.

## Reward and payouts

You earn a fixed amount for every customer you refer who starts a paid subscription. The standard reward is €40 per paying customer, with no limit on the number of customers. If you have a large reach and want to agree a different reward, mention it in your application. The amount on your stats page is the one that applies to you.

A reward first stays pending for 30 days. If the customer keeps paying after that, the amount becomes payable. We pay out monthly by bank transfer.

## Your stats page

Your stats page shows:

- **Your referral link**, with a button to copy it
- **Visits**: how often someone arrived through your link
- **Sign-ups**: workspaces that signed up through your link
- **Paying customers**: workspaces that started a paid subscription
- **Pending**, **Payable** and **Paid out**: your rewards by status
- **Recent sign-ups** with date, amount and status

It contains nothing that lets you identify the customers you referred. You see how many customers came through you, not who they are.

## Reward statuses

- **Pending**: the customer started paying and the 30-day waiting period is still running
- **Payable**: the waiting period has passed and the customer is still paying
- **Paid out**: the reward has been transferred to your bank account
- **Forfeited**: the customer cancelled within the waiting period, or the payment was refunded or charged back before the reward was paid out

## Privacy and tracking

Attribution uses only the code in your link, kept for the length of the browser session. A visit is counted once per session and sends only your code. The visitor is not identified and no personal data is stored.
