# Failed invoice email? Double-check the address

If a customer says they did not receive an invoice email, start by checking the email address stored on their contact record.

## The most common cause

The email was sent, but it bounced because the address is wrong or the inbox is full. Check the customer's **primary email address** in **Customers > Contacts**. Look for typos such as:

- `.cm` instead of `.com`
- `.con` instead of `.com`
- `gmal.com` instead of `gmail.com`
- Missing digits in a phone-style address
- Extra spaces before or after the address

## How to fix and resend

1. Open the customer record
2. Correct the email address
3. Save
4. Open the invoice and click **Send** again

MyCompanyDesk resends to the updated address, and you can preview the email before sending.

## Other things to check

| Issue | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Spam folder** | Ask the customer to check spam/junk |
| **Email provider blocks attachments** | Some providers filter PDF attachments |
| **Different contact should receive invoices** | Use the customer's billing contact if one is set |

## When a signing or quote email fails

The same address problem affects signing-request and quote emails. When MyCompanyDesk cannot deliver a signing request or a quote signing request, the failure is shown on the detail page of the document, contract or quote instead of being reported as sent. Fix the email address on the customer's contact record, then resend the request from the detail page.

## Still not working?

If the address is correct and the email is still not arriving, contact MyCompanyDesk support. Include the invoice or signing-request number, the email address you used, and the date/time you tried to send it.
